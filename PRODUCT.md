# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General audience with emphasis on recruiters, graduate school admissions, research collaborators, and peers reviewing work. They are scanning for credibility, research depth, and engineering ability with limited time.

## Product Purpose

Showcase an AI researcher portfolio with publications, projects, and experience in a clear, impressive, and impactful way. Success signals include interview requests, graduate school opportunities, research collaborations, and strong professional impressions.

## Brand Personality

precise, heavy, risk taker

## Anti-references

None provided. Avoid low-contrast or color-blind inaccessible treatments that obscure content.

## Design Principles

- Evidence-forward credibility (publications and projects lead)
- Hierarchy drives scanning and comprehension
- Contrast-first accessibility for inclusive readability
- Balance researcher depth with builder pragmatism

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize WCAG-style contrast targets and color-blind friendly palettes. Avoid color-only cues for meaning.
