# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A general audience: friends, recruiters, clients, collaborators, graduate admissions and peers. Most arrive
from a link, scan for a few seconds, and decide whether to keep scrolling.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Gading Aditya Perdana (gadingap.com): a generalist who builds iOS and macOS apps and AI
systems, deepest in machine learning (computer vision, vision transformers) and software engineering
(including MLOps and DevOps), who also does UI/UX design (HIG, Gestalt) and field, user and design research.
Studies Computer Science at BINUS University and is an Apple Developer Academy scholar. It should
feel crafted and fun to scroll, show how things are made, and read plainly. Research papers are one part of
the story, not the headline. Success: a first-time visitor scrolls to the end, and the right people get in
touch.

## Brand Personality

precise, heavy, risk taker

## Anti-references

- Glass on content surfaces (glass is for controls, per HIG), or more than one gradient text.
- An academic CV or an industry pitch.
- Decoration that pretends to be evidence (fake detections, invented numbers).

## Design Principles

- Built in layers: everything is a physical layered object that separates and reassembles as you scroll.
- The original blue -> cyan palette and "color bends" background, kept calm enough for text to stay legible.
- Apple HIG and Gestalt principles applied deliberately: hierarchy, grouping, common regions, 44 pt targets.
- Every scene rests on a finished, readable layout; motion animates away from it and back.
- Modest, factual copy; the work speaks.
- Fast and cool: nothing renders while the visitor is idle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG contrast targets in both themes (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text and UI). Never colour-only cues. Real DOM
text in reading order, keyboard-reachable dialogs, reduced motion treated as a first-class design.
